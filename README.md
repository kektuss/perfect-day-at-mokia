# perfect-day-at-mokia 

Objective:
Welcome to the world of a "happy" 9-5 office worker Moka! Every weekday morning, Moka gets to experience a perfect day--loading off stress by asserting dominance on Mokia Future Tech students while handing out jelly cookies. In the prestigious company called Mokia, the towers are divided; Each tower has its own amenities and is assigned to specific workers. The Mokia Future Interns belong to tower 1 but always sneak into Moka's tower to steal the Tower 2 jelly cookies. Help Moka defend their tower and keep the pesky Interns from getting the snacks.

Coding Languague:
Python 

How to Play/ Controls:
Click your trackpad or use your mouse to press on the Mokia Future Tech Interns. They don't have the badge that says they are allowed jelly cookies. The more Interns you catch the better! You are a time crunch, so work efficiently. Good luck!!!
